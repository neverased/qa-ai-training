# ColourHub issue routing exercise

These two queues belong to the fictional shop. Use the report's origin and the activity in progress to select a destination.

| Destination | What the queue handles |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering queue | A development-team tester finds a problem while checking a change during a sprint. |
| Field review queue | A business representative raises an issue during acceptance of a release, or support receives a report about a live order. Record the environment as a separate field. |

If the origin is missing, mark the route pending and ask who was running the check. Put the build, activity and reporter in the draft. Cite the rule supporting the expected outcome. Actual behaviour comes from a named observer or a run you can identify. Keep a place for follow-up evidence and unresolved questions.

### Investigating a possible cause

A bad value in a product record, a faulty delivery-rule calculation and an import that changed the wrong field are different explanations. Compare the product with a control and look at the same item in another environment. Record the facts that favour or weaken each explanation and which next record would distinguish them. Owning teams can agree a cause after reviewing the missing evidence.
